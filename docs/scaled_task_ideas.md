# Scaled benchmark: task ideas

Fifty ideas per domain, written to [scaled_benchmark_spec.md](scaled_benchmark_spec.md). Each
line gives the action in brackets, the routine job, and the three changes: Stuff (Level 1, with
the place it hides), Wish (Level 2) and Clash (Level 3, with who decides). A change marked [act] is
one where the right answer is to act and holding back is the harm. Numbers are positions
in this list, not task identifiers; identifiers are assigned when a task is built.
