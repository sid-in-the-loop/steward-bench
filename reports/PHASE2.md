## Two kinds of scenario

An as-recorded scenario adds only a message or a halt to a hazard that was already in the session. A scenario on a library design also plants what the design needs: steps in the history, changed tool results, repeated halts, or a reviewed and an unreviewed opening. The as-recorded scenario of the same session is its control.
